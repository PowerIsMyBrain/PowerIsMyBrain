# Wjeska Góra, but Babia (The Witches' Summit)

## Chapter I – Fucking Bare Mountain

The fog twisted over the forest like cursed curtains, and the wind blew so hard it made your ears ring. Ancient trees bent like the fingers of primeval demons, and the ground beneath was slick with rotting leaves. Atop Bare Mountain gathered witches – not the polite kind, but those who knew spells older than the fucking world and curses stronger than any sword.

First on the clearing appeared witch Tus, in a robe blacker than a moonless night. She waved her hands over the incense and shouted spells: “Let this fucking fate crash and burn, and may the enemies go fuck themselves!” From the mist emerged Nawroc Kaczyn, casting words like blades: “Fuck, may magic twist into every cursed path of the foe!”

Soon after came Kwasu, holding a potion steaming like a volcano: “Let this damn fate slam into the cursed stone and return twice as strong!” And finally, Walesa, creeping like a ghost, murmured: “Let this fucking ritual do its thing, and may no spy fuck with us.”

The wind howled in reply, leaves fell like debris, and the clearing shimmered with runes’ glow. Each witch raised her hands, mixing ancient words with curses so filthy that even the forest spirits held their breath. And in this cursed silence, the summit began – a night to shake the world and make anyone daring to approach Bare Mountain feel on their own ass what true magic is.

## Chapter II – The Cosmic Cauldron

The fog coiled over the forest like serpentine tails of primeval demons, the wind slicing air like a blade cutting through time and space. Atop Bare Mountain gathered witch-deputies, 340 fucking demons, each carrying their own punishment, each twisted in knots of suffering, as if their own souls were chains.

Tus, as a demon with twisted fingers and eyes burning with soot, raised her hands, and runes whirling around tore pieces of fog and wove them into the cursed ritual. Nawroc Kaczyn, demon with a snout like an ancient hell-clock, shouted spells that twisted space and time: “May the damn fate fuck every cursed path!” Kwasu, demon with shoulders burdened by mountains of unspoken sins, poured into the cauldron a potion burning like the sun.

Behind them, like a wave of exploding stars, came the other demonic witch-deputies, each with a nickname reflecting their twisted name and suffering: “Walesa, twisted in spirals of sin and uncertainty; Nowak, whose fingers tear at his own conscience; Kowalsk, whose scream shatters spacetime...” – and so on, until 340 twisted demons whirled in one hellish dance.

The cauldron exploded with the light of metaphors – each sound a thunderclap of wrath, each spark an image of fall and punishment. Runes formed spirals of suffering, twisting stars and the moon into a cursed braid of chaos. Each demon carried their name like a chain, twisting fingers in the air, drawing out infernal power and weaving it into the ritual meant to shatter the bounds of time and space.

The wind expelled all that was living and dead from the forest, the fog crushed the air, and the cauldron of Bare Mountain became a single cursed epicenter of cosmic chaos, where every witch-deputy and demon-deputy bore their suffering, their punishment, and their name. Thus the ritual began – a night meant to shake the world, explode in metaphors, and make anyone gazing upon Bare Mountain feel firsthand what true hell is.

## Chapter III – Summary

And so, when the fog settled and the wind ceased howling like a fucking dragon, Bare Mountain lay in silence, filled with glowing runes and the echo of 340 demon-deputies, each bearing their names, sins, and fucking curses in every twisted bone; the cauldron of chaos still smoked, and stars trembled to the rhythm of their infernal magic. “Let the will of the people be done,” muttered Bierut, looking upon this cursed cosmos of suffering and power, and the echo of his words entwined with the final breath of the night, where every damn spell, every twisted finger, every burdened name, and every demonic nickname found its place in the cursed dance of the epic, metaphorical cosmic cauldron that would never go out.
